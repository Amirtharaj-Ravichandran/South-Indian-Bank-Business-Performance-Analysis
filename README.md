# South-Indian-Bank-Business-Performance-Analysis
A comprehensive banking business performance analysis focusing on regional target achievement, business growth, product-wise performance, and operational efficiency using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.
# Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of South Indian Bank's business performance, focusing on regional-level target achievement, business growth, product performance and operational efficiency.

The study uses banking business data to evaluate the performance of different Regional Offices (ROs), identify high-performing and underperforming regions, and generate meaningful insights to support business monitoring and strategic decision-making.

Microsoft Excel was used for data preparation, cleaning and preliminary analysis, while Microsoft Power BI was used to develop interactive dashboards and visualise key performance indicators.
# Project Objectives
Analyse regional-level business targets against actual achievements.
Identify the top 10 performing branches based on MTD and YTD growth.
Evaluate regional business performance and contribution.
Analyse Gold Loan target versus achievement.
Examine SMA reduction performance across Regional Offices.
Identify business gaps and areas requiring improvement.
Present data-driven insights through interactive dashboards.
# Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, data preparation, calculations and preliminary analysis.
Microsoft Power BI: Data modelling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboards and visualisation.
Power Query: Data transformation and preparation.
DAX: Performance measures and KPI calculations.

# Business Value
The analysis provides a structured approach to understanding regional banking performance. It helps identify business strengths, recognise performance gaps, monitor product-level achievements and support informed management decisions through data visualisation.
# Conclusion
This project demonstrates the application of data analytics and business intelligence techniques to evaluate South Indian Bank's business performance across various Regional Offices. By utilising Microsoft Excel for data preparation and analytical calculations and Power BI for interactive dashboard development, the study provides meaningful insights into regional business performance, target achievement, MTD and YTD growth, Gold Loan performance, and SMA reduction.

The analysis highlights high-performing regions, identifies business gaps, and provides a comparative understanding of regional contributions to the bank's overall business. The findings demonstrate how data-driven analysis can support performance monitoring, improve operational efficiency, and facilitate informed business decision-making.

Overall, this project showcases the practical integration of data analysis, business intelligence, and banking domain knowledge to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support strategic planning and sustainable business growth.
